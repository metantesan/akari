# Code of conduct

Akari contributors are expected to make participation welcoming and
constructive. Treat people with respect, discuss ideas in good faith, and keep
criticism focused on the work.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, and sustained disruptive
behavior are not acceptable in project spaces. Maintainers may remove content
or restrict participation when needed to protect contributors and keep project
work constructive.

For conduct concerns, contact the repository maintainers through GitHub. For
security vulnerabilities, follow the private reporting process in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
